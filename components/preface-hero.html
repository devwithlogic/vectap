The Procurement Bottleneck in India

From Paper Chaos to Precision AI Verification

Government procurement through GeM historically meant examining thousands of physical pages bound in twine—cross-referencing Udyam certificates, GST return filings, audited balance sheets, and debarment circulars by hand.

VECTA eliminates this 14-day manual paperwork ordeal. It connects in real-time to 12 statutory Government portals, performs automated algorithmic cross-checks, identifies fraudulent submissions, and empowers Procurement Officers with clear decision support.

The Legacy Reality

Physical paper bundles tied in twine, manual fatigue, missed debarments & forged certificates.

The Problem Statement

Procurement officers must cross-check 12+ statutory registrations (GSTN, Udyam, EPFO, ESIC, MCA21, Make in India, CPPP). Manual scrutiny causes 14 to 21-day delays and severe oversight risks.

The AI Solution

AI Document Engine extracts submitted certificates, runs cryptographic verification via DigiLocker PKI, queries live portal APIs, and flags mismatches (e.g. claimed local content vs actual BoM).

Decision Support Mandate

AI serves strictly as an intelligent decision-support system. As mandated under GFR 2017 Rule 153, the ultimate qualification decision resides with the Procurement Officer.
